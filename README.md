# Dual Lens Management Diagnostic

**Two perspectives. Better management insight.**

Dual Lens is a management diagnostic approach that examines a service relationship through two complementary perspectives:

- **Value Lens (outside-in):** Are we creating the value that matters to customers and other stakeholders?
- **Capability Lens (inside-out):** Can the management system consistently and reliably deliver that value?

The purpose is to help identify where further investigation or management attention may be warranted—not to award maturity scores or prescribe generic best practices.

## Available editions

### Quick Look (QL) — Free

A brief, structured exploration of one clearly defined service relationship or management concern.

Designed to help users identify a useful starting point for further inquiry.

**Available for ChatGPT and Claude.**

### Lightweight (LW) — Free

A more detailed, structured diagnostic exploration, suitable when additional discussion, evidence and analysis are needed.

Lightweight goes beyond Quick Look while remaining less extensive than a facilitated FULL diagnostic.

**Available for ChatGPT and Claude.**

## Important operating principles

Dual Lens works best when examining **one clearly defined service relationship or problem boundary at a time**.

Different services may have different value and capability issues. Combining unrelated problems into a single diagnostic can obscure the underlying causes.

Results are intended to support informed judgment and further investigation. AI-generated observations should be reviewed and verified before being used in management decisions.
